Why Horizontal Scroll?

Horizontal scrolling breaks away from conventional vertical navigation, creating a unique and memorable user experience.

  • Perfect for image galleries and portfolios
  • Encourages users to explore more content
  • Creates a sense of progression and journey
  • Ideal for step-by-step processes
  • Works beautifully on mobile with swipe gestures
