Inclusive Motion Design

Respecting user's motion preferences with prefers-reduced-motion media query.

Animated Element

Why It Matters

5-10% of users have vestibular disorders or photosensitivity. Animated content can cause dizziness, nausea, or migraines. Always respect system preferences.

Quick Checklist

  • Use prefers-reduced-motion to pause/dampen animations
  • Provide controls for motion-sensitive users
  • Consider optional "respect motion" toggle in settings
  • Avoid parallax for reduced-motion users
  • Test with screen readers and motion settings
