Why Bento Works

The varying card sizes create a natural reading hierarchy without explicit labels:

  • Larger cards = more important content (featured services, hero stats)
  • Smaller cards = supporting info (quick links, badges, metrics)
  • Color/contrast variation guides the eye naturally
  • Stacks to single column on mobile automatically
