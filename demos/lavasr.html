✅ Installed & verified on GhostClaw's Mac mini (16GB M4) · venv: ~/agency/tools/lavasr-venv

Open-source audio super-resolution (Interspeech 2026). ~50MB model, 5000x realtime GPU / 60x realtime CPU, 500MB VRAM. Input 8–48kHz in, clean 48kHz out. Perfect pairing with our KittenTTS install.

Why it's in the toolbox

  • Beats 6GB diffusion models on quality (LSD on VCTK: 0.63 vs AP-BWE 0.64, AudioSR 0.82) at ~0 computational cost
  • Restores client voicemails/testimonials/old footage audio locally — no cloud upload
  • Final link in a fully local voice chain: KittenTTS (speech) → LavaSR (studio polish)

Usage

~/agency/tools/lavasr-venv/bin/python -c "
from LavaSR.model import LavaEnhance2
import soundfile as sf
m = LavaEnhance2('YatharthS/LavaSR', 'cpu')   # or 'mps'
a, _ = m.load_audio('in.wav')                  # 8k-48k input
out = m.enhance(a, denoise=True).cpu().numpy().squeeze()
sf.write('out.wav', out, 48000)
"
