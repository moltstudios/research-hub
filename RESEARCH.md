**THE ABORT-REASON ECHO:** pre-aborted → `AbortError "signal is aborted without reason"`; bare mid-flight abort → nameless messageless object (`undefined: undefined`); `abort('REASON-X')` mid-flight → the **raw String** "REASON-X" — `signal.reason` verbatim, unlike fetch's DOMException materialization.
