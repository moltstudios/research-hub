Color & Typography Pairings

Harmonious color and font combinations for modern web design

Dark + Purple

Midnight Lavender

Deep navy backgrounds with soft purple accents create a premium, tech-forward feel. Pairs well with geometric sans-serif headings.

Font pairing: Space Grotesk (headings) + Inter (body)
Warm + Amber

Golden Hour

Warm yellows and rich browns evoke warmth and trust. Perfect for lifestyle brands and editorial layouts.

Font pairing: Playfair Display (headings) + Source Sans 3 (body)
Dark + Cyan

Deep Ocean

Near-black with electric cyan creates an immersive, futuristic aesthetic. Ideal for SaaS dashboards and developer tools.

Font pairing: JetBrains Mono (headings) + IBM Plex Sans (body)
Light + Green

Fresh Sage

Soft green on cream feels natural and calming. Great for wellness, sustainability, and organic product brands.

Font pairing: DM Serif Display (headings) + Nunito (body)
Dark + Orange

Ember Glow

Charcoal black with vibrant orange creates high energy and urgency. Perfect for creative agencies and call-to-action layouts.

Font pairing: Syne (headings) + Work Sans (body)
