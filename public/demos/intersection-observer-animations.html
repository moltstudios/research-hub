Why Intersection Observer?

🚀 Performance

Unlike scroll event listeners, Intersection Observer doesn't fire on every scroll event. It's much more efficient and doesn't block the main thread.

🎯 Precision

You can define exactly when to trigger animations (50%, 75%, 100% visible) with the threshold option.

📱 Browser Support

Excellent browser support (95%+ globally) with fallbacks available for older browsers.
