Demo 1: Reveal on Scroll

This section fades in and slides up as you scroll. The animation is driven by the view() timeline, which tracks the element's visibility in the viewport.

Scroll down to see the effect. This pattern is perfect for:

  • Feature sections on landing pages
  • Product showcases
  • Blog post sections

Parallax Background

The background moves slower than the scroll, creating depth and immersion. Uses scroll() timeline.

Demo 2: Sticky Header Transformation

As you scroll past the hero section, the header shrinks, gains a backdrop blur, and becomes solid. This is perfect for improving navigation visibility.

Uses scroll() timeline with animation-range: 0px 200px to only animate after scrolling 200px.

Demo 3: Scroll-Triggered Animation

This is a scroll-triggered animation (Chrome 145+), not scroll-driven. It plays once when the element reaches 25% into its entry phase, then continues on a normal time-based timeline.

Perfect for one-time entrance animations that shouldn't scrub with scroll.

animation-trigger: view();
animation-range: entry 25%;
