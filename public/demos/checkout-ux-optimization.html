Best Practices Checklist

HIGH PRIORITY

Guest Checkout (Critical)

Forcing account creation causes 24% abandonment. Make guest checkout the primary option. Invite account creation post-purchase when the customer is already committed.

HIGH PRIORITY

One-Page Checkout

Reduces abandonment by 20% compared to multi-step. Shoppers see the entire flow before starting, reducing anxiety about how many steps remain.

HIGH PRIORITY

Express Payment Methods

Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Link bypass the entire form. Digital wallets convert 2-3x faster than card entry. 12-15% overall conversion lift.

HIGH PRIORITY

Running Order Total

Display running total throughout checkout (sidebar or sticky header). 48% abandon due to unexpected costs. No hidden fees at final step.

MEDIUM

Inline Validation

Show errors immediately (red border, helper text) not on submit. Prevents frustration from completing forms only to discover errors at the end.

MEDIUM

Trust Signals

Secure badge, payment logos, SSL notice above fold. "Your payment info is secure" near payment fields. Social proof reduces cart anxiety.

MEDIUM

Mobile Optimization

70%+ e-commerce traffic is mobile. Thumb-friendly CTAs bottom-right, 44px touch targets, sticky order summary on scroll.

MEDIUM

Progress Indicators (Multi-Step)

Always show "Step 2 of 3" — ambiguity about remaining steps drives abandonment. Multi-step only for complex shipping/B2B scenarios.

LOW PRIORITY

Post-Purchase Account Creation

Show on order confirmation page: "Create password to track order, view history, get faster checkout next time" — benefit-driven framing.
